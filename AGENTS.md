# Global UX Research Portfolio - Consolidated Brief

## Product Vision

Build a world-class portfolio that positions the owner as a globally competitive UX Researcher capable of attracting recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, speaking opportunities, and freelance clients.

The portfolio should communicate expertise, credibility, strategic thinking, and measurable impact within the first few minutes of interaction.

---

## Problem Statement

Many UX Research portfolios fail because they:

- Focus heavily on aesthetics while neglecting communication
- Present research as a collection of artifacts rather than business outcomes
- Require visitors to read excessively before understanding impact
- Lack clear calls to action
- Fail to establish trust quickly
- Do not differentiate the researcher from hundreds of similar candidates

The result is reduced recruiter engagement and lower interview conversion rates.

---

## Primary Goals

1. **Increase recruiter trust within the first 30 seconds**
2. **Communicate expertise without overwhelming visitors**
3. **Showcase research rigor and strategic thinking**
4. **Demonstrate measurable business and product impact**
5. **Increase inbound opportunities**

---

## Success Metrics

- Visitors understand specialization within 10 seconds
- Visitors reach a case study within 2 clicks or less
- Case study completion rate exceeds industry averages
- Increased interview requests and inbound opportunities
- Reduced bounce rate on homepage visits

## Key Market Intelligence

### 1. Outcome-Based Hiring
Research portfolios are increasingly evaluated on outcomes rather than activities.

**Implication:** Case studies should emphasize impact rather than deliverables.

### 2. Recruiter Attention Span Is Shrinking
Average recruiter review sessions last only a few minutes before deciding whether to continue evaluating.

**Implication:** Homepage must quickly communicate who you are, what you specialize in, why you're credible, and what action visitors should take.

### 3. Strategic Researchers Are Favored Over Tactical Researchers
Organizations increasingly seek researchers who influence product strategy rather than conduct usability studies.

**Implication:** Case studies should highlight decision-making influence, product strategy involvement, prioritization support, and stakeholder alignment.

### 4. Accessibility Is Becoming a Hiring Signal
Accessibility awareness increasingly differentiates senior researchers from junior practitioners.

**Implication:** Accessibility considerations should be visible throughout the portfolio.

### 5. Personal Branding Is Increasingly Important
Researchers who publish content, contribute to communities, speak publicly, or participate in open source stand out.

**Implication:** Community involvement should be showcased prominently.

---

## User Journey

1. **Entry** → Visitor lands on homepage. Goal: Understand who the researcher is and whether they are relevant.

2. **Evaluation** → Visitor scans expertise, credibility indicators, and featured work. Goal: Determine capability and trustworthiness.

3. **Validation** → Visitor reads one or more case studies. Goal: Validate methodology, thinking, and impact.

4. **Conversion** → Visitor downloads resume, schedules conversation, or reaches out. Goal: Generate opportunities.

## Success Criteria

The portfolio succeeds when visitors can:

1. Understand specialization immediately
2. Trust the researcher quickly
3. Validate expertise through evidence
4. Easily initiate contact
5. Remember the researcher after leaving the site

**Ultimate measure:** Increased interview invitations, collaborations, and global opportunities.
